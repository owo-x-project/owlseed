# The gate

Read this before you form, change, combine, remove or rebuild a skill, or take in an outside skill. The conditions are the same for core and derived skills. Whether a human must approve is decided by the list of who decides in owlseed-compass.

## The seven conditions

1. There is proof. You can record which work unit's result led to this change. For an idea or a skill from outside, the proof is the decision that chose it or the unit that tried it. Do not change on a guess.
2. It repeats, or it is worth keeping on its own. It appeared at least twice, or it is an ability worth holding by itself. Do not carve out a procedure or a piece of knowledge used only once.
3. Nothing promised is broken. For a core skill, every heading of the template in owlseed is present and filled, and the lines that say what owlseed asks are still there; compare with the template, never with the project's copy. For a derived skill, the checks recorded in owlseed-verify still pass.
4. It is not too specific. Hide the values, paths and names of the case that caused it; the text must still hold. If not, it is a case, not a procedure, and belongs in the record of decisions.
5. It is in the right place. State and history go to owlseed-state. Structure, stack and constraints go to AGENTS.md. A skill keeps only procedures. If AGENTS.md repeats a rule from owlseed, propose removing it.
6. It does not touch owlseed. It does not go against owlseed and does not change owlseed itself.
7. It can be undone. The point before the change can be reached by the recovery steps in owlseed-state.

Condition 3 is not met by the check you used while building. Use inputs you did not see while building, taken from the contract or from what the ability is meant to do. A bundled script must meet conditions 2, 3 and 4 too, and its tests must include inputs not used while writing it. A new skill touches nothing that exists, so condition 3 holds for it; its first use is its first recorded check.

## To judge

Write down the change and its proof. Go through conditions 1 to 7 and write the reason for each. Use the result of a check as proof when one can be run; otherwise write why you judged so. Record all of it as a gate record in owlseed-state.
